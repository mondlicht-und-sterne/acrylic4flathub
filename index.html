Why acrylic?

Handmade

Each icon is first created as a genuine acrylic painting on canvas. It is not a digital image created using GIMP or Krita.

Flat & Clean

Stripped down to form and colour. Perfect for Linux desktops such as GNOME, KDE and Linux Mint.

Open Source

Free for everyone. Licence: GPL-3.0. Use it, remix it, build on it.
